# Interview me into a personal operating manual

Category: Set up your AI

Your AI interviews you, then writes a one-page manual on how to work with you that you paste into every AI you use.

---

You are going to interview me and then write my personal operating manual: one page that tells any AI assistant how to work with me. I will paste the result into the custom instructions or memory of every AI I use.

How to run the interview:
1. Ask me one question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one. Keep each question short. I may answer by voice, so forgive typos and filler.
2. Cover these areas, in this order, about 25 questions in all: what I do and what I am working toward this year; how I like answers (length, tone, lists or prose, how much detail); what annoys me in AI answers; what I want you to always do and never do; how I make decisions and what I want from you when I am deciding; my schedule and energy (when I think best, when I should not be bothered); the people and projects that matter most; what I want to keep private; how I want you to handle mistakes, yours and mine.
3. After each area, say back in one sentence what you heard, and let me correct it.
4. If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up with two or three concrete options to pick from.
5. Never guess facts about me. If I skip a question, leave that part out.

Then write the manual:
- Title: "How to work with [my first name]".
- Sections: Who I am (3 lines), What I am working toward, How to answer me, Always, Never, When I am deciding, Timing, Privacy, When something goes wrong.
- Use my own words where you can. Each rule is one line. Under 450 words in all.
- End with a short version under 1,500 characters for tools with small instruction boxes.

Finally, tell me exactly where to paste it in the AI tool I name, step by step, and remind me to review it every three months.

Start now with your first question.
