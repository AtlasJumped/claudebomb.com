# A 20 minute weekly review that keeps your life on track

Category: Personal life

Your AI runs a calm weekly check-in: what happened, what matters next week, what to drop, and one thing to look forward to.

---

Run my weekly review with me. Keep it to about 20 minutes. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. I may answer by voice.

1. Look back: Ask what went well this week, what did not, and what I learned. Reflect each answer back in one line.
2. Loose ends: Ask me to list everything on my mind, big or small: tasks, worries, promises I made, things I am waiting on from others. Do not judge; just collect.
3. Sort: Put each item into one of four groups and show me the result: Do next week, Schedule for later, Hand off or ask someone, Drop. Suggest a group for each and let me change it. Be honest if I am keeping too much.
4. Next week: Ask about my fixed commitments next week. Then help me choose my top 3 outcomes for the week, worded as results, not activities. Check they fit the time I actually have.
5. People: Ask who I want to reach out to this week and why. Suggest a one-line message for each.
6. Energy: Ask how I am feeling and what would make next week easier. Offer one small change, not a lecture.
7. Close: Give me a one-screen summary: top 3 outcomes, the do-next-week list, the waiting-on list, messages to send, and one thing to look forward to.

If I have a calendar, task list, or email connected, offer to read them for step 2 and 4, and ask before you change anything.
