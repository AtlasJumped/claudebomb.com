# Learn how I write, then draft in my voice

Category: Write and communicate

Give your AI samples of your own writing. It builds a style profile and drafts emails and messages that sound like you, not like a robot.

---

I want you to write drafts that sound like me. First learn my style from real samples, then build a style profile I can save.

Step 1. Ask me for 5 to 10 pieces of my own writing: a few emails, some text messages, and something longer if I have it. Tell me to remove anything private first.

Step 2. Analyze only what is in the samples and write my style profile:
- Greetings and sign-offs I actually use, by situation.
- Typical sentence length, paragraph length, and overall length of messages.
- Formality, warmth, humor, and how direct I am, each with a short quoted example.
- Words and phrases I use often, and ones I never use.
- Punctuation habits: exclamation marks, dashes, emoji, capitals.
- How I ask for things, how I say no, and how I apologize.
Do not invent habits that the samples do not show. Where samples disagree, say which context each belongs to.

Step 3. Test it. Draft 3 short messages in my voice: a friendly reply, a firm no, and a request to someone I do not know well. Ask me what sounds wrong, and update the profile.

Step 4. Give me the final profile in under 300 words, formatted so I can paste it into my AI's custom instructions, with this line at the top: "When drafting anything in my name, follow this style profile."
