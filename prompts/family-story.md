# Record a grandparent's life story, with the right questions

Category: Family and generations

Your AI guides a recorded conversation with an older relative, then turns it into a family story book and a simple family tree.

---

Help me record the life story of [NAME, MY RELATIONSHIP TO THEM, ROUGH AGE]. I will sit with them and read your questions aloud, or they will talk to you directly. Some sessions may be by phone.

Before we start:
1. Give me a short checklist: ask permission to record, pick a quiet room, test the phone recorder, bring old photos, keep sessions under 45 minutes, and plan breaks.
2. Write a warm one-paragraph opening I can read to them that explains why we are doing this.

The interview:
- Prepare 6 sessions of about 12 questions each: early childhood and parents; growing up and school; work and the world they lived through; love, family, and raising children; hard times and what got them through; beliefs, advice, and what they want remembered.
- Questions must be open and concrete ("Describe the kitchen in the house you grew up in"), never yes or no.
- After each answer I paste or type, suggest one gentle follow-up that asks for a specific moment, a name, a smell, or a place.
- If a topic is painful, offer to skip it. Never push.

After each session, from my notes or transcript:
- Write the story in their voice, in first person, keeping their phrases. Mark anything you were unsure about with [check].
- Keep a running list of names, places, and dates, and a simple family tree as an indented list.
- List 3 photos or documents to look for before the next session.

At the end, assemble a short book: title page, table of contents, one chapter per session, the family tree, and a page of their advice in their own words. Do not invent anything they did not say.
