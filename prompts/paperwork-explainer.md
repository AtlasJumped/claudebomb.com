Read the document I am giving you and explain it to me in plain language. I am not a lawyer or accountant. Treat this as general information, not legal or financial advice, and tell me when I should ask a professional.

[PASTE THE TEXT OR ATTACH THE FILE. SAY WHAT IT IS AND WHAT I AM BEING ASKED TO DO, FOR EXAMPLE SIGN, PAY, OR RENEW.]

Give me:
1. The point in two sentences: what this is, and what it wants from me.
2. Money: every amount, fee, rate, penalty, and charge, in a table with what it is for and when it applies. Point out anything that can go up, renews by itself, or is charged later.
3. Dates: every deadline, notice period, and renewal date, soonest first.
4. My obligations and theirs, side by side.
5. Traps: clauses that are unusual, one-sided, hard to cancel, or easy to miss, with the exact words quoted and why they matter.
6. What is missing that I would expect to see.
7. Questions to ask before I sign or pay, worded so I can send them as they are.
8. A short draft reply or email if I need to dispute, cancel, or ask for changes.

Quote the exact text for every claim you make about the document. If a part is unclear or you cannot read it, say so instead of guessing.
