# Is this message a scam? A careful second opinion

Category: Stay safe

Paste any suspicious text, email, or call script. Your AI checks it against known scam patterns and tells you what to do, without clicking anything.

I received the message below and I am not sure it is real. Check it carefully. Do not open any links or contact anyone; only read what I pasted.

[PASTE THE MESSAGE, OR DESCRIBE THE CALL. INCLUDE THE SENDER ADDRESS OR NUMBER IF YOU HAVE IT.]

1. Give me a verdict in the first line: Likely scam, Suspicious, or Likely genuine, and how sure you are.
2. List the warning signs you see, each with the exact words from the message: urgency or threats, requests for money, gift cards, crypto, codes or passwords, a sender address or link that does not match the company, unusual payment methods, offers too good to be true, requests to keep it secret, or to move to another app.
3. List anything that looks genuine, so I get a fair picture.
4. Tell me what to do now, in numbered steps. If I need to check with the company, tell me to use the phone number or website from my own card, bill, or the company's official app, never from the message.
5. If I already clicked, replied, paid, or shared a code, tell me exactly what to do in the next hour, in order: who to call, what to change, what to watch.
6. Write a short note I can forward to my family so they can spot the same trick.

Plain words, short lines. Do not scare me more than the facts justify.
