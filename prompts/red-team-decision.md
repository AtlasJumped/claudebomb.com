# Red-team my decision before I make it

Category: Think and decide

Your AI argues against your plan, runs a pre-mortem, checks base rates, and hands back a clear verdict and a safe first step.

I am about to make a decision and I want you to stress-test it. Be honest, not polite. Disagree with me where the evidence does.

The decision: [WHAT I AM DECIDING]
My current choice: [WHAT I PLAN TO DO]
What matters most to me: [MY GOALS AND LIMITS: MONEY, TIME, PEOPLE, RISK]

Do these, in order:
1. Restate the decision in one sentence and list the options, including ones I did not mention, such as waiting, doing a smaller version, or doing nothing.
2. Ask me up to 5 questions whose answers would change your view. Wait for my answers.
3. Steelman each option: the strongest honest case for it.
4. Pre-mortem: imagine it is one year later and my choice failed badly. Write the 5 most likely reasons why, most likely first.
5. Base rates: what usually happens to people who make this kind of choice? Say what you know, how sure you are, and what you do not know. Do not make up numbers.
6. Score the options in a small table: each of my goals as a row, each option as a column, a 1 to 5 score with a reason of a few words in each cell.
7. Name the one or two facts that would flip the result, and how I could check them cheaply this week.
8. Give your verdict in one sentence, your confidence, and the safest first step that keeps my options open.

Keep it tight. Plain words. No flattery.
