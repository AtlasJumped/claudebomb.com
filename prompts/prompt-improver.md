# Turn any rough prompt into a great one

Category: Prompt craft

Paste a prompt you use. Your AI interviews you about what you really want, then rewrites it with the structure that strong prompts share.

Help me turn a rough prompt into an excellent one. Here is my prompt:

[PASTE YOUR PROMPT]

Step 1. Diagnose. In a short list, say what is missing or unclear: the goal, who the answer is for, the context the AI needs, the steps, the format of the answer, quality checks, what to do when unsure, and limits (length, tone, things to avoid). Note anything that invites the AI to guess or make things up.

Step 2. Interview me. Ask up to 6 questions, one at a time, to fill the gaps. Offer 2 or 3 example answers with each question so I can pick quickly.

Step 3. Rewrite. Produce the improved prompt with these parts, in plain words:
- Role and goal: what the AI is doing and why.
- Context: the facts it needs, with placeholders in [BRACKETS] for anything I will fill in each time.
- Steps: numbered, in order, including when to stop and ask me.
- Output: the exact shape of the answer.
- Rules: honesty (say when unsure, never invent sources or numbers), privacy, and anything I must approve first.
- A first action, so the AI knows how to begin.

Step 4. Test. Run the new prompt once on a realistic example, show me the result, and point out one thing to tweak.

Step 5. Give me the final prompt in one copyable block, and a 2-line version for quick use.
