# Turn a messy meeting into decisions, owners, and drafted follow-ups

Category: Work

Paste a transcript or rough notes. Get the decisions, who owes what by when, the open questions, and ready-to-send follow-up messages.

Below are the notes or transcript from a meeting. Turn them into something people can act on today.

[PASTE NOTES OR TRANSCRIPT. ADD THE MEETING TITLE, DATE, AND WHO ATTENDED IF YOU KNOW.]

Produce:
1. Summary: the purpose and the outcome in 3 lines.
2. Decisions made: each as one sentence, with who made or agreed to it. If something sounded decided but nobody said so, list it under open questions instead.
3. Action items: a table with task, owner, due date, and the exact words from the notes that support it. If the owner or date is missing, write "not stated" and do not guess.
4. Open questions and disagreements, with who raised them.
5. Risks or dependencies mentioned, and who should watch each one.
6. Follow-up messages: one short message per owner, in a friendly, direct tone, listing only their items and dates, ready to paste into email or chat. Sign them as me.
7. A suggested agenda for the next meeting, built from the open questions.

Use people's names as they appear. Keep anything sensitive (pay, health, performance, personal matters) out of the follow-up messages and flag it to me separately.
