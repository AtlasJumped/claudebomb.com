Why claudebomb

One free place for every prompt, for every AI. Here is why we built it, what we promise, and how it compares.

Search for your prompt now

1. Everyone needs good prompts, and they are scattered

A prompt decides how good an AI's answer is. The AI companies' own galleries cover only their own AI, and Anthropic has turned its prompt library into a guide. Everything else is spread over dozens of sites, each with its own slice.

2. One best place, so you don't have to hunt

One search covers our own prompts and 3,428 free prompts from the open galleries. We also link every other gallery we know of, paid ones too, which none of the sites below does as far as we have seen. If a better prompt is somewhere else, we want you to find it.

3. Updated every day

Every day we pull the openly licensed galleries (prompts.chat and Fabric) and record every prompt that was added, changed or removed. Last checked: November 25, 2026. See what changed. Paid galleries are linked, never copied. We also log the public changes and new versions of every other gallery on our gallery tracker.

4. The best AIs, working on your prompts

We are adding the top model from each leading AI company, so they can check each other's work. We are also training models to write better prompts, and we will show the whole workflow here as it runs. These are being added now, and this page will say when each one is live.

  2. DebateThe top AI from four companies argue out one best version. The council.Being added
  3. TuneEach AI tunes the winner for itself, and the next one checks it.Being added
  4. TrainOur own models learn from every debate to write better prompts.Being added

5. Serious hardware, given to this

Our home server runs two NVIDIA RTX 5090s, the most powerful graphics card made for home PCs. Claude estimated how rare that is: fewer than 1 in 100,000 people own a machine like it (the top 0.001%), and among people who run AI on their own computers, it is roughly the top 1%. Most people with a machine like this use it to make money. We are putting ours to work on prompts, starting with AI search and prompt testing, because we like doing this.

How Claude estimated this

No one publishes how many RTX 5090s exist, so this is an estimate with wide error bars. About 0.41% of computers in the Steam Hardware Survey for October 2026 have one RTX 5090. Steam has about 198 million monthly users, so about 0.8 million cards there. Adding AI and workstation buyers Steam misses gives 0.5 to 2 million cards worldwide.

Two in one machine is much rarer than one. We assumed 1% to 5% of cards sit in two-card home machines (no survey counts this), which gives about 2,500 to 50,000 such machines. Out of about 8.3 billion people, that is at most 1 in 160,000.

For people who run AI at home, estimates of that group run from about 733,000 (r/LocalLLaMA members) to 8.9 million (Ollama's own user count). That puts two RTX 5090s anywhere from the top 0.03% to the top 7%, and roughly the top 1% in the middle case. Spot a better number? Tell us with the HMR button below.

6. Free forever, with no ads

We promise claudebomb will stay 100% free, with no ads, forever. We pay for the AI and the hardware ourselves. Our own prompts are free to use under CC BY 4.0, so they stay free even if we are gone. Our aim is that nobody ever needs another prompt site.
