What it is

Reaction videos, made by you and your people

Watching somebody watch something amazing, or scary, or ridiculous, is fun. Reaction App makes that the whole point. You pick a short clip, you send it to a friend, and their reaction becomes the new content.

Clips are kept short, 5 to 10 seconds, and meant to get a response. You can choose from a library or record your own. The clip plays in a square at the top of the phone while the front camera captures the person watching.

How it works

Three steps

  1. 1

    Pick a clip

    Browse a library sorted by how people react, or record your own. Short, sensational clips keep the reaction fresh.

  2. 2

    Send it

    Choose friends in the app or people from your phone contacts. A friend opens it. The clip plays in a square taking up about half the phone, held upright. After a clear notice and a tap to start, the front camera records. They do not see their own face, so nobody gets self-conscious.

  3. 3

    Get the look back

    When the clip ends, the reaction goes back to you automatically, side by side with the clip. They can always skip recording.

Steps 2 and 3 across two phones need a server. The prototype does the whole loop on one device, so you can feel it today. Choosing from phone contacts uses the Contact Picker API, which works in Chrome and other Chromium browsers on Android. Elsewhere you can type a name or number, or share a link.

Reaction to the reaction

Ping pong

Reactions to reactions

You send a clip. They react. Now you watch their reaction, and your reaction goes back to them. Then theirs comes to you again.

Most rounds end after a couple of turns. If the faces stay funny, it keeps going. You choose when to stop.

Community

The Reaction Wall

The heart of Reaction App is a public board of reactions that people chose to share. Every one has a thumbs up, and every one can be reacted to. React to a reaction, then react to that, and the thread grows.

  • Library

    Find the clip

    Sort by Top rated, your Favorites, Most reactions, Most laughed, Most disgusted, Most angry, Shocked, or Touched.

  • Feelings

    Tag the feeling

    After you react, tap how it felt. Viewers can add tags too, so the library learns what each clip does to people.

  • Threads

    Reactions to reactions

    Open any public reaction and add your own. Threads show who reacted to whom.

  • Leaderboards

    Climb the board

    Most watched reactions, most reacted-to clips, and top per feeling. See today, this week, and all time.

The prototype has Library, Community, Leaderboard, and Thread screens as a design preview. They show no people and no numbers, only Example and n/a, because a server is needed before real users exist. The app is free to use on phones and desktops.

Groups

Everyone watches, then everyone sees everyone

Send one clip to a group. Each person watches on their own time. When the last person finishes, one combined video of all the reactions goes out to the whole group.

Phones show a grid of 1 or 4 reactions at a time. Desktop screens have room for many more, so a big family or a whole class still fits.

Family

Built so grandparents can play too

Reaction App works in a desktop browser as well as on a phone. A grandparent can send a clip from a big screen and see the grandkids' reactions on that same big screen.

No app store trip is needed to start. Open the link, allow the camera when asked, and go.

Privacy and consent

Your camera, your call

  • Clear notice, then a tap

    The camera never records without an on-screen notice and a tap to start.

  • Always skippable

    The person watching can choose to watch without recording, or stop and discard at any time.

  • Nothing is uploaded

    In this prototype there is no server. A reaction stays on your device until you download or share it yourself.

  • Public is a choice

    Each person will choose public or private for each reaction. Private will be the default.

A real version needs consent rules before it ships. That includes parent or guardian consent for minors, and clear consent before any reaction is posted publicly. We have not built those yet, and we will not publish anyone's reaction without them.

About clips: we do not pull videos from YouTube or other platforms, because their terms generally do not allow re-hosting. The real app would use clips people record themselves, licensed libraries, or embeds where a platform allows it. The prototype's clips are original and drawn with code.

What it takes

An honest list before a public board can open

  • A server, storage, and bandwidth

    A public board needs a server, video storage, and bandwidth. These cost money even when the app is free to use.

  • Moderation and reporting

    App stores require reporting and moderation tools for apps with user posts. We have to build them before launch.

  • A minimum age of 13

    Public accounts will need users to be at least 13, with clear sign-up rules.

  • Consent from the person shown

    The person in a reaction must agree before it goes public. Private stays the default.

  • A copyright takedown process

    Uploaded clips can infringe copyright, so we need a way for owners to report a clip and have it removed fast.

Roadmap

Where this goes

  1. NowWeb prototype: clip, hidden-face recording, reaction card, installable on a phone or desktop.
  2. NextAccounts and a server, so a clip can reach a friend and the reaction can come back.
  3. ThenInbox, friends, ping pong, and group videos with 1 or 4 up on phones and more on desktop.
  4. LaterPublic feed with thumbs up, consent rules for minors, and a clip library with proper licenses.
  5. Store releasesFree to start, with paid storage later. An App Store release needs an Apple Developer Program membership (99 USD per year), which the app's owner enrolls in personally. A Google Play release needs a Google Play developer account. Until then, the web app installs from the browser.
