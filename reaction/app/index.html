Pick a clip

Prototype: runs on this device only

Choose a short clip, then watch it while we capture your reaction. Clips are 10 seconds or less.

    All clips here are original and made with code. The real app would use clips people record, licensed libraries, or embeds where a platform allows it. We do not copy videos from other sites.

    On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Ready?

    Tapping React shows a notice first. Your camera stays off until you tap Start.

    Before we start

    • When you tap Start, your camera and microphone record you while the clip plays.
    • You will not see your own face. The recording is for the reaction card only.
    • Nothing is uploaded. It stays on this device until you download or share it.
    • You can skip. Watch without recording, or stop and discard at any time.

    Public posting and minors: a real version needs clear consent rules before any reaction leaves your device, including parent or guardian consent for children. This prototype posts nothing.

    Reaction privacy

    Who can see my reactions

    Private is the only mode today, because nothing leaves your device. In the real app you will choose for each reaction, and Private will stay the default.

    Your data on this device

    The prototype keeps no account and stores nothing. A reaction exists only until you leave the card screen or delete it.

    Storage plan: free to start. Saving lots of reactions will later need a paid membership, because storing video costs money.

    App stores

    Today this installs from your browser. An App Store release needs an Apple Developer Program membership held by the app's owner (99 USD per year). A Google Play release needs a Google Play developer account.

    Sending is coming soon

    Sending to friends, ping pong, and groups all need a server and accounts. We have not built those yet, so nothing is sent from here. What works today is the reaction card on this device.

    • Friend: they open it, watch, and the reaction comes back to you.
    • Ping pong: reactions to reactions, back and forth.
    • Group: everyone watches, then one combined video goes to all.
