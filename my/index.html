    What happens to what you save

    On this device, your prompts stay in this browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

    Once you have an account, we keep your saved prompts so they follow you to any device. We also read saved prompts together, with everyone's, to find changes that make a shared prompt better for everyone. If one of your changes makes it into a shared prompt, we credit you in that prompt's history under the name you choose, or not at all if you leave it blank. You can turn this off for your account.

    No ads, ever. We never sell what you save. Don't save passwords or private details in a prompt.
