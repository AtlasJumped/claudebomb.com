Support us

Our mission: find humanity's problems and build solutions that stay free forever.

In full transparency, we are a very small team that just wants to do good things for humanity. Everyone on the team works a full-time job, and we have no plans to stop. Everything so far has been paid for out of our own pockets, and we plan to keep it that way, but it would be really nice if we did not have to.

Three ways to give

Giving to us is not open yet. When it is, you will pick one of these.

Spend it however you see fit

The piggy bank

Everything goes into a piggy bank that keeps the sites running: hardware, software, the subscriptions we pay for every month, and in the future, people we hire.

Tell us what it is for

Like the piggy bank, everything goes back into the work, but you pick where. Love one of our sites in particular, or just love dogs? Choose this and tell us.

Have you heard of Wikipedia?

We have given to Wikipedia 51 times since August 2021. Humanity needs open, free projects like it to be rewarded so they can keep going forever. If you have heard of Wikipedia, please donate to them or to us.
