## aiformyparents.com

Short version of the message: "Your kids want you to use AI. You don't know how to do it, and it's hard to set up. Here is an easy way to set it up."

How it works, in plain words: you can just chat with AI, or press a button and talk to it. The mistake most people make is sending short sentences like "build me a website". That won't build a very good website. A detailed plan, written over days, would. This site does that work up front: it has the detailed instructions that people have spent days on, so you can copy and paste one big block of text that gives your AI everything it needs. At the end, a button takes you straight to the download page for an AI app.

### Why does this site exist?

My generation has been getting incredible benefits from the blossoming world of artificial intelligence. Many of us want our parents to try it, and they just won't listen to us.

What our parents don't always see is that one reason we want them to try artificial intelligence is that it will bring them closer to us. The disconnect between us is about communication, not about values.

We learned from our parents, and we would like to return it and help you learn. We believe artificial intelligence marks a stage where parents can finally learn from their children. Every generation gets smarter than their parents. When in your life did you decide that your children were smarter than you? You might not have. To believe they never will be is to believe we are a species that does not improve generation over generation.

This site exists because we want to show you that we can help you learn, and that everything we learned from you can be paid back tenfold. In the past, parents always stood on the shoulders of their parents. In this generation, we want parents to stand on our shoulders, and elevate together.

Unless you give it a chance, you'll never know.

### Tailoring

AI can help you with your own parents, help you connect with your kids, and help you be the keeper of family information that connects everybody else. When one person in a family starts using AI and encourages the others, the whole family ends up closer.
