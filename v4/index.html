One dashboard for your whole life

A free, open-source personal dashboard, plus a tool that helps you make it yours. Work, home, family, and fun in one calm place. It works with AI assistants such as Claude.

Get updatesContribute

The dashboard

Everything you do, in one place

Six tabs, one privacy firewall. Connect the apps you already use, and open another window only when you want to. We are building it in the open, so what you see here is the plan, not a finished product.

01

One list for everything

Every task in one place, sorted by time and urgency. Filter by work, home, or your goals.

02

Today, this week, the long view

Day, week, and long-term views across all your calendars. Keep one for home and one for work, and we link them.

03

Start a workflow, not a chore

When you have time to be productive, pick a workflow. With eight emails waiting, we draft all eight replies in your style. You edit and send.

04

Your free time counts too

What you own, events near you, and the things you like to watch, play, and do.

05

Remember the people who matter

Birthdays, contacts, and gift ideas, ready before the day arrives.

06

A page that represents you

One profile you control. It tells your own AI what you like and dislike, and you choose what the world sees.

Privacy

A firewall between work and home

Work stays at work. Home stays home. You set the rules, and nothing crosses the line unless you say so.

Work

Your work email, calendar, and tasks.

Home

Your family, your free time, your profile.

The customizer

Make it fit you

The second half of the product is a tool that helps you customize your own dashboard. Describe what you want, and your AI assistant, such as Claude, helps you shape it. Every improvement you share can help the next person.

Status: in design. Tell us what you would want it to do.

Safety

Tested before you download

Everything we offer for download is screened and tested before we publish it. If it fails, it does not go up. Contributions are reviewed too, and you choose whether yours is public or anonymous.
