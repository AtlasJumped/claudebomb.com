Have you heard of Wikipedia?

We think Wikipedia is a better version of us.

It inspires us. Its work has added a huge amount to humanity's productivity, and it has kept all of it free. We have never had to pay to read it, never had to hand anything over, and never seen an ad on it. It's great, and we have been taking part.
