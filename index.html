6 Vision

2036: everyone's prompt bank

Picture 2036. Humanity has a free, public prompt bank that everyone owns and everyone upgrades. When you have something complicated to ask an AI, this is the first place you go.

One-sentence requests will keep getting easier. A specific workflow will still do better. "Make me a dashboard" gets you a dashboard. A long prompt gets you the exact dashboard, with the features and tools you want. That gap is why this site will stay useful.

7 Why this exists

Our hypothesis

Communication you can customize is what gets people to adopt AI. Most people type a short question and stop there. A great prompt shows the AI who you are, what you want, and how to check its own work.

claudebomb is a free, open, clearly licensed bank of long prompts for everyone, improved in the open. Every prompt here follows the same bar: a clear goal, the context the AI needs, numbered steps, the shape of the answer, honesty rules (say when unsure, never invent sources), and what the AI must ask you before acting.
