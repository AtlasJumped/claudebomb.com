3 Why this exists

Our hypothesis

Communication you can customize is what gets people to adopt AI. Most people type a short question and stop there. A great prompt shows the AI who you are, what you want, and how to check its own work.

What we found when we looked at other prompt sites in November 2026:

  • Most free libraries are short one-line prompts, such as prompts.chat and the Claude Code prompt library.
  • The deep, long prompts mostly sit behind paywalls with unclear rights to use them, such as PromptBase and God of Prompt.
  • Developer tools such as Fabric, LangChain's prompt hub, and agent skills are built for programmers, not for everyone.
  • Anthropic's and Google's own prompt galleries have turned into guides on how to prompt.

claudebomb is the free, open, clearly licensed bank of long prompts for everyone. Every prompt here follows the same bar: a clear goal, the context the AI needs, numbered steps, the shape of the answer, honesty rules (say when unsure, never invent sources), and what the AI must ask you before acting.
