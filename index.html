claudebomb is your helping hand into the blossoming world of artificial intelligence

claudebomb is a piece of plain text. You copy it, paste it into your AI assistant, and within five minutes your AI does three things for you. A dashboard is one simple page that shows your day in one place: your to-do list, your email, your calendar, and the people who matter to you.

Why this exists

Reasons people hesitate to adopt AI

These reasons apply at every level, from a first chat to a daily power user. Everyone is on a separate journey, and people learn the same lessons alone. We think everyone should reach a basic standard that is easy to reach.

You find out after years that you used it wrong

That is the most frustrating one. Files saved in the wrong places, lost context between chats, and money spent on the wrong plan. We learned these lessons the expensive way, so you do not have to.

I don't know how to use it

Most people open a chat box, type a question, and stop there. Nobody showed them what else it can do.

I don't know how to optimize it

Even people who use AI every day cannot tell whether their setup is good. Without a comparison, you cannot tell whether you are doing well or missing something simple.

No app shows how to optimize it

We do not know of an app that looks at how you use your AI and shows you how to use it better. This does, with your own assistant, and it says plainly what it cannot see.

No app shows your usage and models

It is hard to see your own usage, and which models you use for which jobs. The setup asks your assistant to list the accounts, connectors, and models you use and what you do most.

These problems plagued early adopters. The fix is simple: clear structure and instructions from day one.

Automated or manual setup

With automated setup, your assistant does the work for you and you say yes or no to each choice. With manual setup, you pick every step yourself.

Most people should pick automated. Tuning by hand can fine-tune your results, and either way you can say no to anything.

Automation, explained

AI assistants come in several models. Stronger ones are better at hard problems. Smaller ones are faster and cheaper for easy jobs. You do not need to know which is which.

The file tells your assistant to judge how hard each job is and pick the right model for it. This part is optional.

We climbed the hill. This is the rope.

We spent too much money and learned how to save. We ran GPU jobs for days that turned out to be useless. AI has a steep learning curve, and we wrote down what we learned so your assistant can do the climbing for you.

Safety

Is it safe to copy and paste this into my computer?

Yes. Pasting text into a chat can do no more than typing the same words yourself.

  • Text you paste is only words. It cannot run by itself.
  • It inherits the security of your AI. Your AI asks before it creates, moves, sends, or changes anything, and it works only with the connections you have already set up.
  • You can read every line first. Press Read the text at the bottom of the page.
  • It asks for no passwords and sends nothing to us.
