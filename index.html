For comparison, ChatGPT: Go $8 a month, Plus $20 a month, Pro from $100 a month (chatgpt.com/pricing). Claude prices from claude.com/pricing, November 2026. Plans change; check there.

Our experience

We run everything on one $200 plan. Our main assistant, Opus, hands each job to the model that fits: hard problems to the strongest, building to a mid-size model, quick fixes to the smallest. That keeps usage down. On that one plan we have built and run several websites, including this one, without paying anything extra.

This is what happened for us, not a promise about your plan.

Tips to keep usage low

  • Use the smallest model for small jobs.
  • Start a new chat when one gets long.
  • Let one main chat delegate.
  • Keep files small and reuse them.

05 Use case: Social

Your page, your circles, your rules

The dashboard can double as a page about you, so you never need to buy a domain. You choose what is public, who can message you, and who can see where you are.

Your profile page

Public or private, by category. Big switches first, details after. Click Open on any row to fill it in.

0 of 7 public

Pick from hundreds of customizations, the way old social pages let you express yourself.

Who can message whom

  • You
  • Close trusted friends
  • Their close friends
  • Everyone else, needs permission

Messages go straight through between close trusted friends and their close friends. Anything wider needs permission, with a reason attached.

The dashboard may also suggest who to reach out to, such as a friend who loves music.

Share location with best friends

Optional. Off by default. Turn it on person by person.

Sharing with nobody.

If you listen to true crime, you know it's wise to have at least one person who always knows where you are. Share your location with a best friend here, and they will have your history if the police ever need it.

The idea: close friends know you are safe and feel connected. You pick how long history is kept, and only the friends you choose can ever open it.
