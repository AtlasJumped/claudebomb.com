Why this exists

Problems that stop people today

These apply at every level, from a first chat to a daily power user. Each one has the same fix: copy and paste.

I don't know how to use it

Most people open a chat box, type a question, and stop there. Nobody showed them what else it can do.

I don't know how to optimize it

Even people who use AI every day cannot tell whether their setup is good. Without a comparison, you cannot tell whether you are doing well or missing something simple.

You find out after years that you used it wrong

That is the most frustrating one. Files saved in the wrong places, lost context between chats, and money spent on the wrong plan. We learned these lessons the expensive way, so you do not have to.

You cannot see how you use AI

It is hard to see your own usage. The setup asks your assistant to list the accounts and connectors you use and what you do most.

Everyone is on a separate journey

People learn the same lessons alone. We think everyone should reach a basic standard that is easy to reach.

No app shows how to use your AI better

We do not know of an app that looks at how you use your AI and shows how to use it better. This does, with your own assistant, and it says plainly what it cannot see.

Work dashboard

This is the story we are working toward. It is not built, and nothing here is available yet.

  • Sometime in early 2027, someone brings the personal dashboard to their company, and it merges with a company dashboard.

Automated or manual setup

With automated setup, your assistant does the work for you and you say yes or no to each choice. With manual setup, you pick every step yourself.

Most people should pick automated. Tuning by hand can fine-tune your results, and either way you can say no to anything.

Automation, explained

AI assistants come in several models. Stronger ones are better at hard problems. Smaller ones are faster and cheaper for easy jobs. You do not need to know which is which.

The file tells your assistant to judge how hard each job is and pick the right model for it. This part is optional.

We climbed the hill. This is the rope.

Build it with us

Tell us how your work really works

The personal dashboard is here now. The work dashboard is a future state, and we build it here, together. Once people have personal dashboards and see the value, we expect them to say at work, "we need this." We do not plan to be the salespeople. You will be.

How we expect it to go

  1. You build a personal dashboard.
  2. Your company agrees to bring a dashboard to work.
  3. The company sets its policies, and you decide what goes to your work account and what does not.
  4. Once those conditions are set, everything left over is automated, and what you chose to share is there.

What could flow between home and work

  • A sense of community: the names of significant others and pets, birthdays, hobbies, and interests. Share a lot or a little. We hope people share more.
  • Your home dashboard may reach your work calendar and to-do list, so you can add to them from your home computer.
  • Forward a website you find at home to your work computer.
  • If you feel like it, action a work email from home. We want your ideas for more of these that help both sides.
