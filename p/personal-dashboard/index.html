Personal dashboard (flagship)

Audits your AI setup, sets things up from your instructions, and builds one calm page for your day.

Our flagship, and the bar for this site. Free forever.

Read it first

What it does

Paste it into a new chat on day one. Your AI makes you comfortable, checks what it can reach, sets up one tidy folder and a master notes file with your yes, then builds a one-page dashboard for your day: ranked to-dos, inbox with drafted replies, calendar, money, fun, and the people who matter.

Common pitfalls, and how this prompt handles them

Common pitfallAI setups get scattered: a new chat makes a new file and the old one is lost.
How this prompt handles itOne folder and one Read Me Master that every chat reads first.
Common pitfallThe AI acts before you are ready, or sends something in your name.
How this prompt handles itEvery send, buy, delete, or anything permanent needs your yes, every time.
Common pitfallDashboards fill up with made-up sample data.
How this prompt handles itIt shows "not connected" instead of inventing emails, events, or numbers.
Common pitfallYou correct the same mistake again and again.
How this prompt handles itCorrections are written down, and a repeated mistake moves to the top in bold.
