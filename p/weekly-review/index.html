Example answer

Written by Claude from this prompt and a made-up example. Your answer will differ.

See the example

What we gave it: Review done by voice; condensed answers. Went well: finished the grant draft. Not well: skipped the gym. On my mind: reply to landlord about the leak, book car service, Ben owes me the budget numbers, plan Mum's birthday, learn Spanish app streak. Fixed next week: Tue and Thu evening classes. Feeling: tired. Want to reach out to Ben.

Your week on one screen

Top 3 outcomes for next week
1. Landlord has a date to fix the leak.
2. Budget numbers from Ben are in the grant draft.
3. Mum's birthday dinner is booked.

Do next week
Email the landlord about the leak (Monday).
Book the car service.
Book the birthday restaurant.
Two gym sessions, on Monday and Wednesday, since Tuesday and Thursday evenings are taken by class.

Schedule for later
Car service if no slot this week.

Hand off or ask
Budget numbers: ask Ben.

Drop
Spanish app streak, for now. You said you are tired; you are keeping a lot. This one can wait without cost.

Waiting on
Ben: budget numbers.
Landlord: repair date.

Messages to send
Ben: "Hi Ben, could you send the budget numbers by Wednesday so I can finish the grant? Thanks."
Landlord: "Hi, the leak is still there. Could you let me know a repair date this week?"

One small change
Go to bed 30 minutes earlier on class nights.

Something to look forward to
The grant draft is done, and the birthday dinner is coming.
