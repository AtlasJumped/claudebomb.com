What it does

You fill in boxes for your characters, your setting, your style, and your story. Your AI asks what is missing, writes a logline and a shot list, then gives you one finished prompt for any AI video tool, with a list of things the tool must not show.

Common pitfalls, and how this prompt handles them

Common pitfallAI video tools drift: a character changes face or clothes between shots.
How this prompt handles itEach character is described the same way, word for word, every time they appear.
Common pitfallScenes wander outside the place you meant.
How this prompt handles itA decision rule treats any shot outside your setting as an error to fix.
Common pitfallClips run longer than the tool can make, so the end is cut off.
How this prompt handles itLong ideas are split into numbered clips with matching first and last frames.

Example answer

Written by Claude from this prompt and a made-up example. Your answer will differ.

See the example

What we gave it: Characters: Pip, a small red robot with one round blue eye and tank treads; Nana Oak, an elderly woman with white braided hair in a green raincoat. Setting: a rooftop vegetable garden at dawn, light rain. Style: warm stop motion look. Story: Pip shields a seedling from the rain with its arm; Nana Oak brings a tiny umbrella. Length: 15 seconds. No crowds.

Logline
On a rainy rooftop garden at dawn, a small robot shields a seedling until an old woman brings it a tiny umbrella.

Shot list
1. 4 s. Wide, eye level, slow push in. Pip, a small red robot with one round blue eye and tank treads, rolls between the beds. Sound: soft rain, tread squeak. Light: cool blue dawn.
2. 4 s. Close up, low angle, static. Pip raises one arm over a small green seedling. Sound: rain ticking on metal. Light: blue dawn, glint on the eye.
3. 4 s. Medium, eye level, slow pan. Nana Oak, an elderly woman with white braided hair in a green raincoat, walks up holding a tiny yellow umbrella. Sound: footsteps. Light: warm sun starting to show.
4. 3 s. Close up, high angle, static. Nana Oak sets the tiny umbrella over the seedling. Pip's blue eye brightens. Sound: rain on the umbrella. Light: warm golden.

Final prompt
Warm stop motion style, 15 seconds, rooftop vegetable garden at dawn in light rain. Pip, a small red robot with one round blue eye and tank treads, rolls between the beds and raises one arm over a small green seedling. Nana Oak, an elderly woman with white braided hair in a green raincoat, walks up and sets a tiny yellow umbrella over the seedling. Pip, a small red robot with one round blue eye and tank treads, looks up as its eye brightens. Cool blue light turns to warm gold. Sounds: rain, tread squeak, footsteps.

Negative list
No other people, no text on screen, no logos, no street scenes.
