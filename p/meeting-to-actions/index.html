Example answer

Written by Claude from this prompt and a made-up example. Your answer will differ.

See the example

What we gave it: Willow Lane Community Garden committee, 4 May. Attended: Priya, Tom, Jess, me. Notes: water bill up again, Tom will get two quotes for a rain tank by end of month. Jess to post plot rules on noticeboard. Maybe move workdays to Sundays? Priya worried volunteers are dropping off. Tom mentioned he has a hospital appointment next week. Agreed: no new plots until autumn.

1. Summary
Committee met on 4 May about costs, rules and volunteers.
Agreed to pause new plots until autumn.
Water costs and volunteer numbers need follow-up.

2. Decisions
No new plots until autumn. Agreed by the committee.

3. Action items
| Task | Owner | Due | From the notes |
| Get two rain tank quotes | Tom | End of May | "Tom will get two quotes for a rain tank by end of month" |
| Post plot rules on noticeboard | Jess | not stated | "Jess to post plot rules on noticeboard" |

4. Open questions
Move workdays to Sundays? Raised by: not stated.
Volunteers dropping off. Raised by Priya.

5. Risks
Rising water bill. Watcher: Tom, via the quotes.
Volunteer drop off. Watcher: Priya.

6. Follow-up messages
To Tom: Hi Tom, thanks for taking on the rain tank. Could you send two quotes by the end of May? Cheers, [your name]
To Jess: Hi Jess, thanks for posting the plot rules on the noticeboard. Let me know when they are up. Cheers, [your name]

7. Next agenda
1. Rain tank quotes
2. Sunday workdays: yes or no
3. Ideas to keep volunteers

Flagged to you only: Tom's hospital appointment is personal, so I kept it out of the messages. His deadline may need flexibility.
