Example answer

Written by Claude from this prompt and a made-up example. Your answer will differ.

See the example

What we gave it: My grandmother, Rosa Delgado, about 86. Some sessions will be by phone because she lives in another state. She grew up on a farm and loves talking about cooking.

Before we start: checklist
1. Ask Rosa if she is happy to be recorded, and record her yes.
2. Pick a quiet room. Turn off the TV and radio.
3. Test the phone recorder for 30 seconds and play it back.
4. Ask her to keep old photos near the phone.
5. Keep each session under 45 minutes.
6. Plan a short break halfway.

Opening you can read to her
Grandma, I want to keep your stories for all of us, and for the great grandchildren who will want to know where they come from. There are no right answers. Just tell it the way you remember it, and we can skip anything you would rather not talk about.

Session 1: early childhood and parents (first 6 of 12)
1. Describe the house on the farm where you grew up.
2. What did the kitchen smell like on a busy cooking day?
3. Tell me about your mother. What did her hands look like when she worked?
4. What chores did you do before school?
5. Describe a meal you remember from when you were small.
6. What did your father do in the evenings?

After each answer you type in, I will suggest one gentle follow-up about a specific moment, name or place. If a topic is painful, I will offer to skip it.

After each session I will write her story in her voice, marking anything unsure with [check].
