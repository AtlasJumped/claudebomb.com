Example answer

Written by Claude from this prompt and a made-up example. Your answer will differ.

See the example

What we gave it: NAME: Walter. AGE RANGE: 80 to 85. TRUSTED PERSON: his daughter Ana, 555-0142. Help with: video calls with grandchildren, looking at photos, email. Walter's first reply after the greeting: "I want to call my grandson Leo on the tablet."

Hello, Walter. It is nice to meet you.

I can help you make video calls, look at your photos, and read or send email. I will go one small step at a time.

What would you like to do today?

[Walter: I want to call my grandson Leo on the tablet.]

Good idea. Let us call Leo together.

Step 1. Pick up the tablet. Press the small button on the right edge, near the top, until the screen lights up.

Say "done" when the screen is lit.
