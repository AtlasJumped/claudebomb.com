Research

A research brief that grades its own sources

Your AI researches a question the way a careful analyst would: it separates strong evidence from weak, shows where sources disagree, and says what is still unknown.

What it does

Your AI researches a question like a careful analyst: it grades each source, separates strong evidence from weak, shows where sources disagree, and says plainly what is still unknown.

Common pitfalls, and how this prompt handles them

Common pitfallMade-up sources and quotes.
How this prompt handles itIt must name real sources and say when it cannot check one.
Common pitfallAll evidence treated as equal.
How this prompt handles itEach source gets a strength grade with a reason.
Common pitfallA neat answer that hides real disagreement.
How this prompt handles itDisagreements and unknowns get their own sections.
