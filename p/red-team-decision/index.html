What it does

Your AI restates your decision, asks what would change its view, makes the strongest case for each option, runs a pre-mortem, checks what usually happens, scores the options against your goals, and gives a verdict with a safe first step.

Common pitfalls, and how this prompt handles them

Common pitfallAsking an AI about a plan gets cheerful agreement.
How this prompt handles itIt is told to be honest, not polite, and to disagree where the evidence does.
Common pitfallOnly two options get compared.
How this prompt handles itIt adds options you missed, such as waiting, a smaller version, or doing nothing.
Common pitfallConfident numbers that are made up.
How this prompt handles itIt says how sure it is and never invents numbers.
