To install on iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How to play

  • Drag back anywhere on the field to aim, then let go to fire. The dotted line shows where the shot will go.
  • You get three shots per level. Reach the goal mark on the destruction bar to clear it. If you miss, retry as often as you like.
  • Struts push and cables pull. Cut the cables or snap the struts and the whole thing falls. Total destruction scores the most.
  • Points fill the special meter. When it is full, pick a power: dragons, dogs, ants, or drones.
  • Each level makes your weapons stronger and the structure tougher. Tents come first, then wood, steel cable, and carbon.
  • Keyboard: arrows aim, Space fires, 1 to 4 pick weapons, S opens special powers, R retries, M toggles sound.
