Ancient Egyptians Casually Use Modern Tech: Six-Vignette Comedy Montage (20s Video)

From prompts.chat. Contributed by f. Public domain under CC0 1.0, copied from this saved version of its prompt list.

Open the source

The prompt

20 seconds, horizontal 16:9. A photorealistic live-action comedy montage consisting of exactly SIX separate vignettes. Ancient Egyptians casually use modern technology as if it has always existed.

Six shots total, in the exact order below. Each vignette is ONE continuous shot, with hard cuts only at 00:03, 00:06, 00:09, 00:12 and 00:16. No transitions or additional shots. Begin each scene with the characters already positioned for its action. Show one clear gag, then cut after its payoff. Normal real-time movement, no slow motion.

CHARACTERS
Egyptian men: adults aged 25-45, olive-to-brown skin, dark eyes with black kohl, shaved heads or blue-and-gold striped nemes headdresses. Bare chests, white linen knee-length shendyt kilts and brown leather sandals. Real human actors with natural proportions.

Queen, scene 3 only: approximately 28, olive skin, dark almond-shaped eyes, black shoulder-length bob with a blunt fringe, dramatic black eyeliner, turquoise eyeshadow. Thin gold diadem with a small upright cobra, broad gold-and-lapis collar, gold upper-arm cuffs and gold sandals. Fitted ankle-length white pleated linen dress. Calm regal expression. Both hands stay on the jet ski handlebars.

Market woman, scene 4 only: approximately 35, long thin black braids reaching her shoulder blades, kohl eyeliner, plain ankle-length white linen dress, leather sandals. Turquoise-and-gold beaded collar with a gold scarab pendant hanging at upper-chest height. Visibly different from the queen.

Mummy, scene 6 only: adult human figure completely wrapped in aged beige-grey linen bandages, with small frayed ends and dark gaps at the eyes. No exposed face.

All fabric is opaque and securely fitted. Preserve each character's appearance and clothing within their scene.

SETTING
Ancient Egypt during its flourishing period. Stone temples, painted columns, palms, the Nile, working animals and ancient market stalls. Completed monuments look maintained and freshly finished. Scene 2 is an active construction site with unfinished stonework.

No modern architecture, roads, cables, tourists or modern background clothing.

Exactly one visible modern device in each scene:
1. Electric scooter.
2. Forklift.
3. Jet ski.
4. Payment terminal.
5. Cordless leaf blower.
6. Smartphone.

The scarab pendant contains an invisible NFC chip but looks entirely ancient. No additional visible modern accessories or devices. All modern devices are unbranded.

LOOK AND CAMERA
Realistic cinema-camera footage with restrained handheld documentary character. Subtle hand movement, natural motion blur, slight grain, realistic skin pores, sweat, linen fibres, weathered wood and stone dust. Prioritize clear readable actions and stable object shapes. No deliberate focus hunting during a gag.

Outdoor scenes share hard midday sunlight, a clear sky, short shadows and natural reflected light from sand, stone and water. The market awning creates believable shade. Natural colours, restrained contrast, no glossy commercial lighting.

The tomb is illuminated only by one clay oil lamp and the smartphone while its screen is on. Expose the image so the mummy's final movement remains visible in the lamp light.

Camera operators and filming equipment never appear.

TIMELINE

00:00-00:03 | ELECTRIC SCOOTER
One side three-quarter tracking shot at waist height on a stone-paved avenue lined with two rows of ram-headed sphinxes. A temple pylon stands in the distance.

At the opening, a man wearing a nemes headdress is already riding a matte-black electric kick scooter toward screen right. Both feet are on the deck and both hands hold the handlebars. The scooter travels at a brisk but controlled speed over closely fitted paving stones.

He overtakes a walking man leading a camel on the right side of the avenue, leaving a clear safe gap. The camel turns its head to follow the scooter. The rider stays casually serious.

Keep the scooter wheels and the camel visible during the pass. Linen and headdress flaps move naturally in the breeze. Cut immediately after the scooter clears the camel.

00:03-00:06 | FORKLIFT
One steady medium-wide shot at a pyramid construction site. A half-built pyramid rises in the background, with a small group of workers waiting beside slack hauling ropes.

A yellow industrial forklift faces a wooden stone-hauling sledge. A roughly one-metre limestone cube rests on two thick wooden support runners on the sledge, leaving clear channels beneath the stone.

At the opening, the forklift is stationary and both forks are already inserted fully through these channels beneath the block. A shaved-head Egyptian driver raises the block approximately 30 centimetres clear of the supports. The heavy block remains level and stable. A small amount of dust falls from its edges.

Beside the forklift, outside the load's path, a foreman watches the successful lift and makes one short mark on a papyrus scroll with a reed pen.

Show the lift and the foreman's reaction in the same composition. Cut after his mark. No driving, turning or additional lifting.

00:06-00:09 | JET SKI
One wide shot from a low riverbank position, looking across the Nile. Palms and the opposite bank form the background, with a distant pyramid on the horizon.

The queen is already riding a white-and-gold sit-down jet ski from screen left to screen right at a brisk speed. She sits securely astride the seat, her dress arranged clear of the controls and water intake. Her fitted diadem stays secure; only the tips of her bob and the edges of the dress move in the wind.

A small papyrus-reed fishing boat floats several metres to the side of her route. Two fishermen are aboard, one seated and one crouched while holding a fishing net. The jet ski passes without contact and continues toward screen right.

Its nearby trailing wake reaches the boat within the shot. The boat tilts once. The crouching fisherman drops backward onto the boat's low seat and accidentally releases the net's loose edge into the water. The seated fisherman grips the gunwale.

The queen remains composed and does not look back. Keep the passing jet ski and the boat's reaction readable in the same shot. Cut on the fisherman's landing; the boat may still be rocking.

00:09-00:12 | CONTACTLESS SCARAB
One medium shot beneath a linen market awning. Flatbreads, dates, onions and clay jars cover a wooden stall. Soft background movement from shoppers and a donkey carrying baskets.

A bald merchant already holds a small black contactless payment terminal near the market woman's upper chest. His other hand already supports two flatbreads.

The woman lifts her gold scarab pendant a short distance from her chest and touches it once to the terminal's upper contactless reader. The necklace is long enough for the movement and does not stretch. The pendant has a concealed NFC chip, with no visible modern markings.

The terminal shows a simple green confirmation light and makes one beep. No readable words are needed.

She releases the pendant, which falls back against her collar. The merchant immediately extends the two flatbreads, and she takes them with both hands. Both remain entirely matter-of-fact.

Cut as the bread transfer finishes.

00:12-00:16 | LEAF BLOWER
One medium-wide shot in a temple courtyard with painted stone columns.

A priest wearing a nemes headdress stands to screen left with an orange cordless leaf blower. A thin layer of loose dry sand covers the paved ground directly beside the paws of a large stone sphinx.

A second priest is already entering the background to screen right, carrying an incense bowl.

The first priest points the nozzle low and toward screen right. The blower pushes the loose surface sand off the paving. A low plume travels approximately two metres in the established direction and briefly envelops the passing priest's upper body.

The second priest stops. A light dusting remains on his face, shoulders and chest, leaving his expression clearly visible. He keeps hold of the incense bowl and gives the first priest a long, offended stare. A few grains fall from his brow.

The first priest notices him, releases the trigger and lowers the nozzle. The motor winds down quickly. Quiet courtyard ambience remains audible.

Cut on their awkward eye contact. No additional blast or repeated reaction.

00:16-00:20 | MUMMY'S BATTERY
One continuous elevated medium shot looking diagonally into an open granite sarcophagus. A gentle handheld move closer is allowed, without changing the viewing side.

The mummy is already seated INSIDE the sarcophagus, with its legs extended along the interior and its back raised against the head end. This position allows it to recline directly backward without climbing or turning.

One clay oil lamp burns on the floor to the left, close enough to cast warm flickering light across the mummy's upper body and the inner rim. Painted hieroglyphs are faintly visible on the chamber wall.

00:16-00:17.3:
The mummy scrolls once with a bandaged thumb. Hold the phone at an angle where its screen and the mummy's upper body are both readable. Cold screen light illuminates the bandages.

A simple red battery icon with "1%" appears on the phone screen, accompanied by a brief low-battery chime. This is an in-world phone display, not a video overlay.

00:17.3-00:18.3:
The mummy pauses, drops its shoulders and gives a tired muffled sigh. It presses the phone's side button once, turning the display off.

00:18.3-00:19.6:
Still holding the phone, it reclines backward into the sarcophagus. Its head settles naturally against the inner headrest. It places the dark phone flat on its chest and rests its hands beside it.

00:19.6-00:20:
Brief final hold on the mummy lying motionless, with the dark phone visible on its chest in the warm oil-lamp light. The sarcophagus remains open. End.

AUDIO
Only diegetic sound synchronized with visible actions. No music, narration, dialogue, laugh track or added comedy effects.

Scene 1: quiet electric motor, wheels over stone, camel grunt and market-distance ambience.
Scene 2: forklift motor and hydraulics, falling grit, faint construction ambience.
Scene 3: jet ski engine, water spray, wake slapping the reed hull, one brief startled fisherman vocalization.
Scene 4: subdued market chatter without intelligible words, one payment beep, bread and fabric movement.
Scene 5: blower motor and sand hiss, then the motor winding down and quiet courtyard ambience.
Scene 6: faint phone taps, one battery chime, muffled sigh and bandage rustle. Very quiet tomb ambience.

CONTINUITY AND PRIORITIES
Exactly six vignettes, each shown once, in the specified order. Total duration exactly 20 seconds.

Each vignette has one main gag. Prioritize its visible setup, action and reaction over background detail. No extra business, repeated attempts or inserted close-ups.

Maintain spatial positions and movement direction within each shot. Hands retain believable contact with controls and props. All vehicles remain supported by the appropriate surface. The forklift block remains stable on both forks. The jet ski never touches the fishing boat. The blower's sand follows the nozzle direction.

The queen and market woman remain two distinct people with different hairstyles and jewellery.

The phone remains in the mummy's hand until it is placed on the chest. Its screen stays dark after the side button is pressed. The oil lamp stays lit, and the sarcophagus never closes.

Photorealistic actors, environments and effects. No cartoon appearance, plastic skin, duplicated people, body deformation, teleportation, changing costumes, floating props or impossible object intersections.

No captions, subtitles, logos, watermarks or recording interface. The only required readable text is the small "1%" on the smartphone screen.
